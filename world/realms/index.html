The Realms

The Realms are the mythic territories of SmiLeeWorld — symbolic zones, narrative regions, and conceptual landscapes shaped by memory, wandering, and the SmiLee persona.

Each Realm represents a different facet of the universe: emotional, symbolic, geographic, or mythic. Together, they form the backbone of the world’s internal logic.

Primary Realms

The Lake Realm

A reflective, liminal space tied to Zürichsee — a Realm of memory, stillness, and transformation.

The Forest Realm

A wandering zone of mythic encounters, solitude, and symbolic thresholds.

The City Realm

Built from Zürich’s international youth culture — a Realm of movement, community, and lived history.

The Dreaming Realm

A symbolic, surreal territory where mythic imagery and lived experience merge.
