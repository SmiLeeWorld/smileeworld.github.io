Photography

Photography in SmiLeeWorld spans vérité documentation, youth‑culture archives, symbolic landscapes, and mythic portraiture. These images form a visual record of lived experience — Zürich nights, lakeside wanderings, international friendships, and the evolving identity of the SmiLee persona.

Below is a growing collection of photographic works that shape the visual memory of the universe.

Featured Photography

Zürich Youth Culture

Early‑2000s documentary images capturing lakeside nights, train stations, and the international youth communities of the Zürichsee region.

Portrait Studies

Intimate portraits exploring identity, presence, and the mythic undertones of everyday encounters.

Symbolic Landscapes

Forests, ruins, and liminal spaces photographed through the lens of the SmiLee persona — half real, half mythic.
