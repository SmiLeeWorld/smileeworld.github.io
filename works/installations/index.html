Installations

Installations are the physical manifestations of SmiLeeWorld — constructed environments, set pieces, symbolic objects, and immersive builds that extend the mythos into real space. These works merge world‑building, design, and narrative architecture into tangible form.

Major Installations

The Enterprise Bridge

A full‑scale recreation and reinterpretation of the iconic sci‑fi command deck — blending fandom, craftsmanship, and mythic symbolism.

Sci‑Fi World Museum

A curated physical space housing props, artifacts, and constructed worlds — a living museum of imagination and narrative history.

Early Zürich Installations

Lakeside constructions, symbolic objects, and early spatial experiments created during the formative Zürich years.

Mythic Objects

Physical artifacts tied to the Codex — symbolic items, crafted pieces, and world‑objects that anchor the mythos.
