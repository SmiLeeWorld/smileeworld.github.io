Film & Video

The film work of SmiLeeWorld spans vérité documentary, mythic‑cinematic hybrids, experimental shorts, and long‑form observational pieces. These films explore youth culture, wandering identities, spiritual encounters, and the mythic textures hidden within everyday life.

Below is a growing collection of films, projects, and cinematic fragments that form the visual backbone of the SmiLeeWorld universe.

Featured Works

Boogie's Night

A vérité‑style documentary capturing a single night of youth, music, and fleeting connection in Zürich’s underground scene.

Crossroads (with Tim Russ)

A collaborative cinematic moment exploring identity, performance, and the quiet intersections of creative lives.

Wanderer

A mythic short film following the SmiLee persona through forests, ruins, and liminal spaces — half documentary, half dream.
