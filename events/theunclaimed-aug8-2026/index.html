About the night

The Unclaimed, one of the early Los Angeles garage‑psych revival bands founded by the late Shelley Ganz, play a rare, intimate Portland show at The Six on Belmont. Known for their fuzz‑charged sound and deep roots in the pre‑Paisley Underground scene, the band continues today through longtime members Patrick R. Cleary, John Worley, and Shaun Bryant, who carry forward the original spark with grit, humor, and heart.

Formed in the late ’70s, The Unclaimed helped shape the modern garage‑psych resurgence with their raw, Nuggets‑inspired energy and cult‑favorite early singles. Their appearances have always been infrequent, giving each show the feeling of a rediscovery—a glimpse into a living lineage of West Coast underground rock.

The band also has a new EP on the way, adding fresh momentum to this appearance and marking a new chapter in their long‑running story.
