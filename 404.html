Lost in the Realms

404 — The Portal Has Not Yet Manifested

You’ve wandered into a region of SmiLeeWorld that technically *exists*, but only in the same way a half‑remembered dream exists — which is to say, not very reliably.

The glyphs have been whispering about this place for ages, but every time someone tries to map it, the map catches fire, apologizes, and quietly folds itself into a swan.

A portal *is* forming — or at least that’s what it claims — but right now it’s behaving like a moody teenager who refuses to come downstairs until it has “finished becoming transcendent.”

The seals are circling, the runes are arguing about whose turn it is to glow, and the whole realm is in that awkward phase between “under construction” and “having an existential crisis.”

So for now, this path remains unmanifested.
Don’t worry — you haven’t broken anything. This realm simply hasn’t decided what it wants to be yet. Some places take longer to hatch.
