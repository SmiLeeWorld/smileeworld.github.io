Epochs

The Epochs are the mythic timeline of SmiLeeWorld — distinct eras marked by shifts in identity, geography, symbolism, and narrative tone. Each Epoch represents a phase of transformation in the life of the SmiLee persona and the world around him.

Primary Epochs

The Zürich Dawn

The formative era — lakeside nights, international school culture, early wandering, and the first emergence of the SmiLee persona.

The Liminal Drift

A transitional period defined by movement between cities, identities, and symbolic landscapes — the beginning of mythic layering.

The Mythic Bloom

The era when symbols, glyphs, and codex structures crystallize — the world becomes both documentary and mythic at once.

The Wandering Continuum

The ongoing present — a fluid, evolving era where the persona, the world, and the mythos continue to expand.
