Passages

MYTHIC TEXTS OF THE SMILEEWORLD

The Passages are the written backbone of the Mythos — short, potent texts that capture the voice, philosophy, and wandering presence of the SmiLee persona. Each Passage is a fragment of the Codex, a moment of clarity, or a mythic utterance tied to a place, an epoch, or a symbolic threshold.

This page gathers the foundational Passages, beginning with Passage 001 (p001v2), the canonical opening of the Wanderful Wizard Codex.

Collected Passages

Passage 001 — The Coastal Wizard (p001v2)

The canonical opening of the Codex. A windswept introduction to the wandering wizard, standing at the edge of land and sea, trousers flapping like a flag of defiance.

Passage 002 — The Lake at Dusk

A reflective fragment tied to Zürichsee — still water, drifting thoughts, and the quiet mythic hum beneath everyday life.

Passage 003 — The Rooftop Oath

A vow spoken into the night air above the city — part solitude, part declaration, part transformation.

Passage 004 — The Forest Spiral

A recursive meditation written after walking the same wooded path for years, each time discovering something new.
