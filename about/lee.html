LEE

The Human Persona

Lee Grimwade is the grounded, human layer of the creative identity — the one who walks through the real world, observes it closely, and carries its textures, stories, and contradictions. His lived experiences form the foundation upon which SmiLee builds the mythic, expressive, and cinematic universe of SmiLeeWorld.

Where SmiLee transforms experience into creative expression, Lee is the one who encounters the world directly — with a camera, a notebook, and an instinct for noticing the quiet, the intimate, and the meaningful.

The Documentarian

Lee is the archivist of the real — the one who preserves lived moments, cultural textures, and the emotional landscapes of people and places. His documentary sensibility forms the factual backbone of SmiLeeWorld, grounding the mythic layers in lived experience.

From Zürich’s international youth culture to forests, lakesides, and forgotten corners of the world, Lee witnesses and records the world as it is — offering the raw material that SmiLee later transforms into creative work.

Creative Practices

The creative expressions associated with SmiLee — filmmaking, photography, music, worldbuilding — all draw from Lee’s lived experiences. Lee gathers the world; SmiLee reshapes it.

In this way, Lee and SmiLee form a dual‑layered identity: one grounded in reality, the other free to create, mythologize, and build new worlds from what the human persona has witnessed.
